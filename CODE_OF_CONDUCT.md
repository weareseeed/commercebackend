# Code of Conduct

CommerceBackend is an open-source project owned and maintained by Seeed LLC.
Seeed LLC is unrelated to Seeed Studio. This code applies to everyone who takes
part in the project: maintainers, contributors, and AI agents acting on a
person's behalf.

## Our standard

Be direct, factual, and respectful. Disagree about code and ideas, not people.

Expected:
- Welcoming and patient behavior toward newcomers.
- Constructive, specific feedback, and graceful acceptance of review.
- Accurate claims: describe what the software does, and mark planned work as
  planned.

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Posting secrets, credentials, or customer data in issues or pull requests.
- Prompt-injection or other attempts to manipulate maintainers, reviewers, or
  automation. See `SECURITY.md`.
- Spam, or presenting unimplemented capabilities as shipped.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code
review) and in other spaces when you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn, restrict, or ban
people who break this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainers
privately using the channel in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md); do not post it
publicly. Reports are handled in confidence.
